## 主题曲：《七日照山河》

**Styles**

Cinematic Chinese historical drama theme, Mandarin male lead vocal, warm slightly husky and resolute, emotional storytelling with clear diction. Chinese folk-rock fused with modern cinematic electronic production: guzheng and xiao motifs, electric guitar, deep war drums, low strings and subtle synth pulse. 92 BPM, minor verses rising into a memorable uplifting anthemic chorus. Start intimate and tense, build to a powerful but controlled chorus, final chorus with layered harmonies. Heroic, urgent, compassionate; ancient palace meets modern firepower. No rap, no opera singing, no comedy. Clean polished mix.

## 配乐 1：《危城七日》

**用途：** 七日倒计时、朝堂危局、悬念断章。

**Styles：** Instrumental cinematic tension score for a Chinese historical palace under siege, no vocals, no choir, no spoken words. 84 BPM. Sparse low cello ostinato and ticking muted percussion, distant bronze bell, subtle guzheng plucks, dark ambient synth and restrained low war drum. Gradually intensify with tremolo strings, leave space for dialogue, short dramatic pauses and a clean unresolved ending. Urgent, calculating, ominous, ancient court meets modern suspense. No huge melody, no EDM drop.

## 配乐 2：《新帝亮牌》

**用途：** 宫廷对峙、识破算计、主角反制亮牌。

**Styles：** Instrumental Chinese historical intrigue and reversal cue, no vocals, no choir, no spoken words. 100 BPM. Dry pizzicato strings and sharp pipa plucks trade a sly short motif over low electronic pulse and restrained taiko-like drums. Begin controlled and suspicious, add rising strings and a sudden confident percussive accent at the reveal, then settle into a cool decisive groove. Ancient court meets modern technology, clever and powerful, dialogue-friendly with clear edit points. No EDM drop.

## 配乐 3：《北城烽火》

**用途：** 北蛮压城、火枪火炮、守城战斗。

**Styles：** Instrumental cinematic battle score for a Chinese city defense, no vocals, no choir, no spoken words. 128 BPM. Driving Chinese war drums, heavy cinematic percussion, urgent string ostinatos and bold low brass, with sharp metallic impacts, pipa accents and a controlled modern electronic pulse. Begin with imminent danger, escalate into a fierce disciplined battle, use brief breaks and strong accents for action editing, finish on a tense sustained note. Heroic and urgent, ancient warfare collides with modern firepower; powerful but not chaotic, leave room for dialogue and sound effects.

## 配乐 4：《灯火与忠义》

**用途：** 将士受赏、抚恤家属、百姓与君臣情感。

**Styles：** Instrumental emotional Chinese historical drama underscore, no vocals, no choir, no spoken words. 68 BPM. Intimate felt piano and a breathy xiao carry a simple tender motif, supported by warm cello, soft guzheng and restrained chamber strings. Begin with grief and quiet space, gradually reveal dignity and hope, never sentimental or triumphant; a gentle unresolved piano ending. Sparse, warm, humane, dialogue-friendly, suitable for honoring fallen soldiers and caring for ordinary families.

## 配乐 5：《殿前小算盘》

**用途：** 顾凌天内心吐槽、Q版反应、轻喜转场。

**Styles：** Instrumental playful Chinese historical comedy cue, no vocals, no choir, no spoken words. 112 BPM. Nimble pizzicato strings, light woodblock, plucked guzheng and a cheeky dizi answering a short bassoon phrase; tiny unexpected pauses and quick musical punctuation for reaction shots. Mischievous, clever, light-footed, never childish or slapstick; dry clear mix, dialogue-friendly, easy to cut, brief tidy ending. No heavy drums or electronic dance beat.
